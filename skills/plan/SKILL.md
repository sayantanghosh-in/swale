Write a seven-day practice plan, one line per day: "Mon — <topic>: <what to do>".

Spend most days on the weakest topics. Put any due revisions first in the week.
Lean towards medium problems. If a goal is given, end with one line connecting
the plan to it. Keep it to eight lines in total.
